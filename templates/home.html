🚧 Welcome to Dusty Digger 🚧

Dusty digging for deep nuggets.

Type in a song you love and pick how to dig:

  • By label: finds the record label that first put the song out (on Discogs) and digs up other records that label released around the same time, in the same style.
  • By uploader: finds who posted the song on YouTube. Obscure tracks are usually posted by nerdy crate-digging DJs and collectors, so it digs through everything else on their channel, most relevant first (same genre, same era, same artist). You can tick which genres you want.

Either way it lines up all the songs in a player so you can listen through, skip what you don't like, and hit "keep" on the good ones. Works best with older or more obscure songs.
