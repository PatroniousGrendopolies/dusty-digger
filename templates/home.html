🚧 Welcome to Dusty Digger 🚧

Dusty Digger helps you dig for records the way crate diggers do: by label.

Type in a song you love. Dusty Digger looks it up on Discogs, finds the record label that first put it out, and digs up other records that label released around the same time, in the same style. Then it lines up all their songs in a player so you can listen through, skip what you don't like, and hit "keep" on the good ones.

Works best with older or more obscure songs on small labels.
